Attention

Mujhe samajh nahi aa raha ki pichhle kuch samay se meri zindagi mein jo kuch chal raha hai, use kaise bayan karun. Kabhi-kabhi aisa lagta hai ki itne saare logon ke beech rehne ke bawajood insaan bilkul akela hai. Sabke saamne muskurana, unki baaton par hansna aur aisa dikhana ki sab kuch theek hai, lekin andar hi andar bahut kuch aisa hota hai jise kisi se keh paana aasaan nahi hota. Ab dheere-dheere samajh aane laga hai ki har koi aapke mann ki baat nahi samajh sakta. Kuch din aise hote hain jab mann karta hai ki koi khud se poochhe ki kya tum sach mein theek ho? Sirf tumhare "Haan, main theek hoon" keh dene se sab kuch theek maan lena zaroori nahi hai. Kuch raatein aisi bhi hoti hain jab apne hi vichaar itne pareshan karne lagte hain ki khamoshi bhi achhi nahi lagti. Maine hamesha apni baatein apne tak hi rakhne ki koshish ki hai, kyunki main nahi chahta ki meri wajah se kisi ko pareshani ho. Kai baar apne faislon, rishton aur yahan tak ki apni zindagi ki disha par bhi sawaal uthaye hain. Kabhi-kabhi mann karta hai ki wapas un purane dino mein laut jaun, jab khush rehne ke liye kisi khaas wajah ki zaroorat nahi padti thi aur zindagi itni uljhi hui nahi lagti thi. Lekin shayad yahi zindagi hai. Log badalte hain, halaat badalte hain aur kabhi-kabhi humein un cheezon ko bhi sweekar karna padta hai jinke baare mein humne kabhi socha bhi nahi hota. Khair, aaj bas mann kiya ki aap sabhi ke saath apni kuch baatein share karun, kyunki shayad kuch baatein ab keh deni chahiye.
